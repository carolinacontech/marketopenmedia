Google Maps SEO vs. Traditional SEO Strategy: What's the Difference?

Market Open Media · Updated September 24, 2026

Owners hear "SEO" and assume it's one thing. For a home service business, it's really two different jobs that happen to overlap — here's what separates them.

Google Maps SEO optimizes your Google Business Profile to rank in the local 3-pack and Maps results — the block with the map and three business listings. Traditional SEO optimizes your website's pages and content to rank in the regular organic ("blue link") results below it. They target different real estate on the same search results page, and for most contractors, Maps SEO drives more direct calls.

What Each One Is

Google Maps SEO is the work of ranking your Google Business Profile in the Map Pack: optimizing categories, service descriptions, service areas, photos, and reviews. It shows up whenever someone searches with local intent — "plumber near me," "roofer in [city]."

Traditional SEO is the work of ranking your actual website pages in organic search — keyword research, on-page content, site structure, backlinks. It captures a wider range of searches, including ones with no obvious local intent, like "how to know if you need a new AC unit."

They're Not Independent

Your website isn't irrelevant to your Maps ranking. Google explicitly uses your website to help understand and confirm what your Google Business Profile is claiming — the services, locations, and business information need to match. A weak, outdated, or thin website can quietly hold back your Maps ranking even if your profile itself looks complete.

That's why our Google Maps SEO work includes website relevance and content alignment — it's not purely a Google Business Profile task.

Which One Should a Contractor Prioritize?

For most home service businesses, Google Maps SEO produces more direct calls, faster, because it captures the highest-intent local searches. Traditional SEO builds a slower, broader kind of visibility — useful, but rarely where a contractor's next job comes from. If you have to choose where to focus first, Maps SEO is almost always the better starting point; traditional SEO is worth layering in once the fundamentals are solid.

FAQs

Is Google Maps SEO the same thing as regular SEO?
No. Google Maps SEO optimizes your Google Business Profile to rank in the local 3-pack and Maps results. Traditional SEO optimizes your website's pages and content to rank in the regular organic search results below the map.

Do I need traditional website SEO if I already rank on Google Maps?
For most contractors, Google Maps SEO drives more direct calls because it targets high-intent, local searches. Traditional SEO still matters because your website's relevance and content directly support your Google Business Profile's Maps ranking — the two aren't independent.
