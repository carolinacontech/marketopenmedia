# Pricing — Market Open Media

Market Open Media does not use fixed public pricing tiers. Pricing is customized per client based on service area size, competition level, and which services are selected (Google Maps SEO, Google LSA management, or both).

## How pricing works

1. **7-Day Trial** — Prospective clients start with a 7-day trial to see the Google Maps SEO process in action before committing to an ongoing plan.
2. **Custom monthly plan** — After the trial, pricing is quoted based on the contractor's market, service area, and scope (SEO only, LSA management only, or a combined plan).
3. **No long-term contracts implied on the trial** — the trial itself carries no ongoing commitment.

## Services covered by pricing

- Google Maps SEO (organic local search ranking)
- Google Local Services Ads (LSA) management (pay-per-qualified-lead)
