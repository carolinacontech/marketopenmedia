How Many Google Reviews Do You Need to Rank on Google Maps?

Market Open Media · Updated September 24, 2026

Contractors ask this constantly. The honest answer is more nuanced than a number.

Google has never published a minimum review count needed to rank in the Map Pack. Reviews feed into "prominence" — one of Google's three official local ranking factors — but there's no disclosed threshold. What matters more than hitting a specific number is having a steady flow of recent, positive reviews compared to the businesses you're competing against.

What Google Actually Says

Google's own guidance states plainly that "more reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." That's the extent of Google's official statement — no count, no percentage, no formula. Any specific number you see quoted elsewhere ("you need 40 reviews to rank") is a third-party estimate, not something Google has confirmed.

Source: Google Business Profile Help

What Matters More Than the Count

  • Relative to competitors: ranking is a competition — what matters is how your review count and rating compare to the other businesses showing up for the same search, not an absolute number.
  • Recency: a steady trickle of new reviews signals an active business. A pile of reviews from years ago, with nothing recent, is a weaker signal than fewer reviews earned consistently.
  • Rating: volume without a solid average rating won't help as much — Google explicitly ties "positive ratings" to the benefit, not review count alone.
  • Responses: Google also recommends responding to reviews as part of a healthy profile.

What This Means in Practice

Chasing an arbitrary review number is the wrong goal. The right goal is a review pace that keeps you competitive against whoever is currently ranking above you, with a rating that holds up. This is part of what we track and build as part of Google Maps SEO management.
