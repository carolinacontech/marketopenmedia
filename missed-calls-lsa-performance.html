Do Missed Calls Hurt Your Local Services Ads Performance?

Market Open Media · Updated October 10, 2026

A missed call isn't just a lost job. It can cost you future leads, and since October 1, 2026, it can cost you money too.

Yes. Google's own ranking documentation lists responsiveness to inquiries as part of the "likelihood of generating a lead" factor in Local Services Ads ranking, and explicitly states that missed calls count against that score. A pattern of missed calls can mean fewer leads delivered to you going forward, not just the one you missed.

Do I Get Charged for a Call I Miss?

Yes, in many cases. Since October 1, 2026, Google charges Local Services Ads advertisers for missed calls received during business hours when the caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. Before this change, a missed call only became billable if the business later reached the customer.

  • Phone menus: if callers must press a key to reach a department, the 20-second timer starts at the keypress. No keypress, no charge.
  • Follow-up calls: if the first contact didn't qualify as a charged lead, later calls that meet Google's valid-lead criteria can be charged. Reporting on the change says advertisers are charged only once for follow-up calls they make to the same person within 15 days.

Source note: this change was announced in an email from Google's Local Services Ads team and reported by Search Engine Roundtable and PPC Land. We did not find a public Google help page for it at the time of writing, and the notice did not describe a dispute process. Check your own account and Google's current help pages for how charges are handled.

Why Google Weighs This So Heavily

Google's Local Services Ads exist to connect searchers with businesses that will actually answer. If a business consistently misses calls, Google's algorithm treats that as a signal the business is less likely to convert future leads — and adjusts delivery accordingly. This is separate from, and in addition to, the mention of response time as part of profile quality.

Source: Google Local Services Help — "About ad rankings"

What Actually Reduces Missed Calls

  • Call tracking — you can't fix what you can't see. Knowing exactly which calls were missed, and when, is the starting point.
  • A clear answer plan for after-hours and busy periods, instead of letting calls go to voicemail by default.
  • Prompt follow-up on any missed call — a callback within minutes recovers a meaningful share of jobs that would otherwise be lost entirely, on top of the ranking impact.

What This Means in Practice

Missed call tracking and reporting is part of how we manage every Local Services Ads account — not as an add-on, but because it directly affects both the job you're missing right now and the leads Google sends you next.
